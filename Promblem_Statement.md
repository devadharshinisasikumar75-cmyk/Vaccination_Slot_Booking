# Problem Statement

## 1. Project Title
Vaccination Slot Booking and Tracking Platform

## 2. Domain
Healthcare

## 3. Problem Statement
Many healthcare centers still rely on manual processes or disconnected systems to manage vaccination appointments and records. This often leads to overbooking, missed appointments, long waiting times, and difficulty tracking vaccination history. Patients also face challenges in finding available slots and receiving timely reminders.

## 4. Proposed Solution
Develop a web-based Vaccination Slot Booking and Tracking Platform that enables patients to book vaccination appointments online, allows healthcare staff to manage schedules efficiently, and maintains a centralized record of vaccination history with appointment reminders.

## 5. Target Users
- Admin
- Healthcare Staff
- Patients

## 6. Objectives
- Enable online vaccination slot booking.
- Maintain digital vaccination records.
- Reduce waiting time and appointment conflicts.
- Send appointment reminders.
- Track vaccination history and status.

## 7. Core Features
- User Registration and Login
- Vaccination Slot Booking
- Appointment Management
- Vaccination Record Tracking
- Notification and Reminder System
- Admin Dashboard
- Reports

## 8. Core Entities / Database Tables
- Users
- Patients
- Vaccines
- Vaccination_Slots
- Appointments
- Vaccination_Records
- Notifications

## 9. User Roles

### Admin
- Manage users
- Manage vaccination centers and slots
- View reports

### Healthcare Staff
- Verify appointments
- Update vaccination status
- Manage patient records

### Patient
- Register/Login
- Book or cancel slots
- View vaccination history
- Receive reminders

## 10. Success Criteria
- Easy online slot booking
- Accurate vaccination tracking
- Reduced waiting time
- Secure patient record management
- Improved appointment scheduling

## 11. Out of Scope
- Online payment
- Medicine delivery
- Telemedicine consultation
